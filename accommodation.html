Stow Maries

Prince of Wales

Affectionately known as POW!

Our personal favourite

This family-run country pub has four ensuite double rooms set around a courtyard. Standard rooms have either a shower or bath, while the premium room has a super-king bed, wet room and freestanding bath.

10/10 breakfast

Please note that guests under 16 cannot stay in the accommodation.

Ferry Boat Inn

Six contemporary ensuite rooms sit in an annexe behind the inn. King-size beds can be converted into twins, with free parking, EV charging and Wi-Fi. Dog-friendly and accessible rooms are available on request.

Getting around

This is a rural area. Please pre-book taxis well in advance.

Transport can be almost impossible to arrange on the night, so booking ahead will help secure availability and a fair fare.
