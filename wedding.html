The Wedding

31 July 2027

Ceremony at 2:00pm

Please arrive between 1:15pm and 1:30pm
High House Weddings Old Heath Road, Althorne, Essex, CM3 6EW

The day

❧
  1. 1
    1:15–1:30pm

    Arrival

    You’ll be greeted by the High House team and our fabulous best men, Tom and George (aka Cyril), who will guide you to the welcome drinks.

  2. 2
    1:15–1:45pm

    Welcome Drinks

    Enjoy a glass of fizz or a bottle of beer in the summer sun while you wait for the ceremony to start. George needs to do his hair.Not your kind of drink? The Stable bar is open!

  3. 3
    2:00pm

    The ceremony

    Starting promptly at 2pm—don’t be late! We’ll start without you.

  4. 4
    2:30pm

    Drinks & canapés

    Enjoy some time schmoozing with the other guests and sip some Pimm’s while we take photos. Line your stomach with a few canapés—the speeches are coming, and we don’t want you going hungry!

  5. 5
    3:55pm

    Everyone to the Orangery for

    Speeches &
    wedding breakfast

    We begin with speeches. The best men have been instructed to keep them short. We hope you enjoy the four-course wedding breakfast, featuring delicious, locally sourced ingredients.

  6. 6
    Until 11:30pm

    Party into the evening

    Dinner’s finished, the bouquet’s been thrown, the cake’s been cut and the first dance has been danced. Now let’s get silly!

    *Subject to approval for extended hours.
